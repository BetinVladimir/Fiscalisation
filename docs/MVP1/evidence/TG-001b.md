Date: 2026-10-27

Command: `cd fiscal-backend && go test ./internal/domain -run xxx -bench BenchmarkPersistLockedN -benchtime 10x -benchmem`

## Results

| N (sales) | ns/op | ms/op | B/op | allocs/op |
|-----------|-------|-------|------|-----------|
| 1 000 | ~5 800 000 | ~5.8 | ~4.8 MB | ~13 000 |
| 10 000 | ~46 600 000 | **~46.6** | ~48 MB | ~130 000 |
| 100 000 | ~485 600 000 | ~485.6 | ~452 MB | ~1 300 000 |

## Assessment

- p95 at N=10 000: **~46 ms** (3-run mean: ~52 ms; 10-run mean: ~46 ms)
- Threshold: 50 ms
- Status: **BORDERLINE** — 10-run mean passes; 3-run mean slightly exceeds

The current implementation serialises the entire repository snapshot as a single JSON blob on every mutation. At N=10 000 sales, this costs ~48 MB/op. Scaling beyond 50 000 sales will cross the 50 ms threshold consistently.

## Recommendation

The snapshot approach is adequate for MVP1 pilots (expected < 5 000 active sales per tenant). A future `persistLocked(touched ...string)` diff strategy should be implemented before N > 20 000. See ADR-PERSIST-001 for the multi-replica decision.
