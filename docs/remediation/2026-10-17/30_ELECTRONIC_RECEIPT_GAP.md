# Electronic fiscal receipt scope gap — 2026-10-19

The product scope declared electronic_fiscal_receipts=true in commit2823e9d. Annex trace row29.24 still said NOT_APPLICABLE, while no corresponding electronic receipt acquisition/delivery lifecycle was found in current Fiscal domain/API. Scope remains enabled as an intended requirement; implementation status is now IMPLEMENTATION_REQUIRED, Annex row FAIL and production blocked. Paper receipt success, rendered sale summaries and emailed software documents do not prove a device-issued electronic fiscal receipt.

Required implementation: model-specific fiscal device capability/profile; authentic receipt acquisition with immutable sale/operation/device/receipt identity and content hash; durable receipt artifact and retention; authorized recipient selection and access; idempotent delivery queue with explicit sent/failed/unknown states; recipient-visible original receipt and controlled paper fallback; export and audit coverage. Legal applicability and exact required delivery/retention rules must be verified against current official requirements before freezing acceptance.

Required automated tests: unsupported device mode rejected before monetary I/O; real protocol byte fixtures for electronic receipt acquisition; mismatched receipt/sale/device/hash refusal; loss of acquisition/delivery response without second fiscalization; process restart and duplicate webhook/delivery; recipient/tenant isolation, expired access, sensitive data handling; durable original artifact retrieval/export; corrupt/missing artifact; paper fallback without duplicate sale. Physical tests must demonstrate actual device-generated content, delivery and recovery on supported Android/iOS/transport matrix. External legal/device approval remains separate.

This is an added tracked implementation gap, not an exclusion from the goal. The trace consistency checker accepts an honest blocked FAIL; passing that checker is not electronic receipt implementation acceptance.
