- `openapi-corrections-v1.yaml` — SHA-256 `03b3da61dee0b66513b1dea23f47749bbc283deb3f22377b1d1e55bbc612a0a0`; OpenAPI Overlay 1.0 with a machine-gated inventory of 20 corrections: Fiscal `SaleLine` omitted the N-18 line discount, `FiscalReceipt` omitted the immutable fiscal-memory/device snapshot, `ComplianceExportRequest` omitted immutable sale-time location/device filters, `BleSession` omitted its mandatory tenant/location/register binding and confused the Edge identity with the bound fiscal device, the canonical `createWebhookEndpoint` `201` response omitted the one-time secret body, nine administrative command bodies incorrectly required server-owned `ResourceMeta`, and five MiniPOS command bodies incorrectly reused complete response resources. Generated clients and request/response validators use the effective overlaid contract while the locked canonical bytes remain unchanged.

The lock is intentionally explicit: changing any source requires a reviewed contract diff, regenerated clients and an update of these hashes. REST, webhook and BLE payloads must reference the canonical schemas rather than introduce transport-specific business models. The runtime completion document may only close implementation-surface gaps; it cannot redefine a business schema already owned by the regulatory contract. The corrections overlay is checked against an ordered allowlist of exact JSONPath targets, required-field inventories and the still-present locked canonical defects.

`contracts/generated/openapi-public-v1.d.ts` and `openapi-runtime-v1.d.ts` are reproducible derived artifacts generated by pinned `openapi-typescript` 7.13.0. `make generate-openapi` refreshes them; `make contract-test` regenerates into an isolated temporary directory, requires byte-for-byte equality, compiles typed `openapi-fetch` client factories and verifies that every canonical/runtime `operationId` exists in the generated surface. Generated files never override the locked YAML sources.
